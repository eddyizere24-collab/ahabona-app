Amabwiriza y’Ibanga

INYANDIKO Y’AGATEGANYO: Iyi ni inyandiko y’ibanze igomba gusuzumwa no kwemezwa n’umunyamategeko ubifitiye ubumenyi mu mategeko y’u Rwanda. Izina ry’uwufite inshingano z’amategeko n’amakuru ye yo kumuvugisha bigomba kuzuzwa mbere yo gutangira kwakira abantu ku mugaragaro.

1. Uwushinzwe amakuru yawe

Mbere yo gutangira ku mugaragaro, AHABONA igomba kugaragaza izina ry’umuntu cyangwa urwego rushinzwe gukoresha no kurinda amakuru, hamwe n’amakuru yizewe yo kumuvugisha. Kugeza icyo gihe, iyi nyandiko ntiraba iya nyuma.

2. Amakuru dushobora gukusanya

Konti: email, nimero ya telefoni n’ijambobanga ribikwa nk’agaciro karinzwe (hash), aho kubikwa uko waranditse. Umwirondoro: izina, akarere, aho utanga serivisi, ubushobozi wahisemo, amafoto wahisemo gushyiraho, ibisobanuro, amashuri, indimi, igihe ushobora gukora n’amakuru yo kukuvugisha wahisemo kongeraho. Itariki y’amavuko n’igitsina ni amahitamo. Ifishi isanzwe yo kwiyandikisha ntigusaba nimero y’indangamuntu, kandi AHABONA ntitanga serivisi yo kugenzura indangamuntu muri iyi verisiyo. Sisitemu ishobora kandi kubika ikimenyetso cy’igikoresho, imyirondoro yerekanwe cyangwa yafunguwe, ibimenyetso by’inyungu, ibirego watanze n’amagambo yo gushakisha.

3. Impamvu dukoresha ayo makuru

Dukoresha amakuru kugira ngo dufungure konti, tugaragaze umwirondoro wawe ku bantu bashaka ubushobozi ufite, tunoze uko ibisubizo bigaragara, turinde urubuga, dusuzume ibirego kandi twubahirize amategeko abigenga.

4. Amakuru abandi bashobora kubona

Abandi bashobora kubona izina ryawe, akarere, ubushobozi, amafoto n’ibindi bisobanuro washyize ku mugaragaro. Nimero ya telefoni igaragara gusa iyo wabyemeye. Email, ijambobanga n’amakuru y’ibanga ya konti ntibigaragazwa ku bandi bakoresha.

5. Ikimenyetso cy’igikoresho n’uko imyirondoro igaragara

AHABONA ishobora gukoresha ikimenyetso cy’igikoresho kitagaragaza izina ryawe kugira ngo imenye imyirondoro yamaze kukwereka, iyafunguwe n’ibimenyetso by’inyungu. Ibi bifasha kuringaniza amahirwe no gutuma ibisubizo bijyana n’ibyo ushaka. Aya makuru agomba gukoreshwa mu rwego rwo gutanga serivisi no kurinda urubuga, atari ukugurisha amakuru yawe.

6. Gusangiza abandi amakuru

AHABONA ntiyagurisha amakuru bwite. Abatanga serivisi zo kubika urubuga cyangwa kohereza email bashobora gutunganya amakuru akenewe kugira ngo serivisi ikore, iyo ibyo bikoreshejwe. Amakuru ashobora gutangwa igihe amategeko abiteganya cyangwa urwego rubifitiye ububasha rubisabye mu buryo bwemewe n’amategeko.

7. Igihe amakuru abikwa no gusiba konti

Iyo usabye gusiba konti, umwirondoro uhishwa kandi konti ishobora gusubizwa mu gihe cy’amezi 6. Iyo icyo gihe kirangiye, amakuru ya konti n’amafoto ayifitanye isano bisibwa hakurikijwe imikorere ya sisitemu. Amwe mu makuru ajyanye n’ibirego cyangwa umutekano ashobora kubikwa igihe gito gikenewe kugira ngo ikibazo gikurikiranwe cyangwa amategeko yubahirizwe.

8. Uko turinda amakuru

Ijambobanga ririndwa hakoreshejwe scrypt n’agaciro kihariye (salt). Cookies za session zashyizweho ibiranga umutekano ku buryo buboneka mu igenamiterere ry’urubuga. Sisitemu igenzura amakuru yoherezwa kandi igashyiraho imipaka ku bikorwa bimwe. Nta sisitemu ishobora kwemezwa ko idashobora na rimwe kugira ikibazo cy’umutekano.

9. Uburenganzira bwawe

Ushobora guhindura amakuru yawe yo ku mwirondoro no gusaba gusiba konti ukoresheje ibiri muri urubuga. Ushobora kandi gusaba amakuru yerekeye amakuru yawe cyangwa uko akoreshwa. Uburyo bwo kutwandikira bugomba kugaragazwa hano mbere yo gutangira ibikorwa ku mugaragaro.

10. Abana

AHABONA igenewe abantu bafite imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga.

11. Impinduka

Amabwiriza y’Ibanga ashobora guhinduka uko urubuga rugenda rutera imbere. Impinduka zikomeye zigomba kumenyeshwa abakoresha kandi inyandiko ikagaragaza igihe zatangiriyeho gukurikizwa.

AHABONA Privacy Notice

DRAFT NOTICE: This initial document must be reviewed and approved by a lawyer qualified in Rwandan law. The name and contact details of the person or entity responsible for personal data must be completed before the website is publicly launched.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Before public launch, AHABONA must identify the person or entity responsible for using and protecting personal information and provide reliable contact details. Until then, this notice is not final.

2. Information we may collect

Account information: email, phone number and a protected password value (hash), rather than the password in plain text. Profile information: name, district, service areas, selected skills, photos you choose to upload, description, education, languages, availability and any additional contact details you choose to provide. Date of birth and gender are optional. The standard registration form does not ask for a national identity number, and this version does not provide identity verification. The system may also store a device identifier, profiles shown to or opened by you, interest signals, reports you submit and search terms.

3. Why we use this information

We use information to create accounts, display your profile to people looking for your skills, improve how results are shown, protect the website, review reports and comply with applicable laws.

4. Information other people may see

Other users may see your name, district, skills, photos and other details you choose to make public. Your phone number is shown only when you have agreed to its visibility. Your email, password and confidential account information are not shown to other users.

5. Device identifier and profile display

AHABONA may use a device identifier that does not directly show your name to keep track of profiles already shown to you, profiles opened and signals of interest. This helps distribute opportunities and make results more relevant. These data should be used to provide and protect the service, not to sell your information.

6. Sharing information with others

AHABONA does not sell personal information. Providers that host the website or send email may process the information needed to provide those services, where those providers are used. Information may be disclosed when required by law or requested lawfully by a competent authority.

7. Retention and account deletion

When you request account deletion, your profile is hidden and the account can be restored within six months. After that period, account information and related photos are deleted according to the system's operation. Some information about reports or security may be retained for the limited time needed to investigate an issue or comply with the law.

8. How we protect information

Passwords are protected using scrypt and a unique salt. Session cookies use the security attributes configured for the website. The system validates submitted information and limits certain actions. No system can be guaranteed to be completely free of security incidents.

9. Your rights

You can update your profile information and request account deletion using the website's available features. You may also ask for information about your personal data or how it is used. Contact details for making such requests must be provided here before public launch.

10. Children

AHABONA is intended for people aged 18 or older.

11. Changes to this notice

This Privacy Notice may change as the website develops. Significant changes should be communicated to users, and the notice should show the date they take effect.
